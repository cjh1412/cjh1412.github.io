About

I am a researcher at the Island Research Center, Ministry of Natural Resources (Fujian, China). I received my Ph.D. in Cartography and Geographic Information Systems from the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, including a joint-training period at Texas A&M University funded by the China Scholarship Council, and my B.Sc. in Geology from China University of Geosciences (Beijing).

My research bridges marine / island spatial planning and quantitative geohazard science. I combine remote sensing, InSAR surface-deformation monitoring, and machine learning to assess coastal and island hazards and to support territorial spatial planning and disaster risk reduction. My current work focuses on refined assessment of coastal geo-environmental stability, integrating multi-source remote sensing and InSAR with machine-learning models.

I am open to opportunities abroad — postdoctoral positions, research-scientist roles, and applied R&D in geospatial science — as well as research collaborations. If my work resonates with yours, I would be glad to hear from you.

Coastal Hazard & Risk Assessment

Geohazard susceptibility, hazard and risk assessment for coastal zones and islands using multi-source data.

🛰️

InSAR Deformation Monitoring

D-/PS-/SBAS-/DS-InSAR for landslides, reclamation areas, and post-eruption island stability.

Geospatial Machine Learning

Random forest, SVM and neural networks for shipwreck susceptibility, DEM error, and susceptibility mapping.

🪨

Rock Mechanics & Microfracture

Shale anisotropy, micro-/nano-fracture characterization, and granite fracture evolution.
